========================================================
1. PRODUCT
========================================================

Product name:

CS2 AI COACH

Main platform:

Telegram Bot + Telegram Mini App

Purpose:

Create an AI-powered CS2 coach that connects to a user's FACEIT
account, detects the user's upcoming/ongoing matches, analyzes
the opponent and match statistics, follows the match state,
and provides adaptive tactical recommendations.

The product should feel like having a professional CS2 coach
sitting next to the player.

The AI must:

- analyze before the match
- analyze map veto/pick information when available
- analyze teams
- analyze players
- analyze recent statistics
- analyze opponent tendencies
- predict likely outcomes
- create tactical plans
- analyze every round when live game-state data is available
- identify mistakes
- learn opponent behavior
- update recommendations
- tell the player what to do next
- provide post-match analysis
- create personalized training recommendations

REAL-TIME ADAPTIVE AI COACHING.

↓

↓

Groq trigger examples:

- major economy change
- major opponent pattern detected
- side switch
- timeout
- clutch situation
- match end

But round-end decisions can have priority.

Cache repeated context.

Use short structured prompts.

Use compact JSON.
